# Grade 8 Gymnasium Physics curriculum and legacy cards

The package follows the [Bavarian Grade 8 Physics curriculum](https://www.lehrplanplus.bayern.de/fachlehrplan/gymnasium/8/physik), retrieved on 2026-11-28. [coverage.json](../physik-gymnasium-8/coverage.json) records the SHA-256 of the HTML, the hours per section and all 33 competence statements in their published order. Its lesson mappings distinguish work implemented in the app from activities for which the app provides preparation or support.

The 17 common lessons build on the light and circuit topics from [Natur und Technik 7](https://www.lehrplanplus.bayern.de/fachlehrplan/gymnasium/7/nt_gym). The curriculum gives 14 hours each to electric current and optics and 28 to mechanics, split into 17 for dynamics and 11 for forces. The NTG path adds Section 4.1 (10 hours) and offers a project on two-dimensional sports motion for Section 4.2 (18 hours). Schools select the topics for Section 4.2 and may choose related topics from technology. The scope note in the coverage matrix lists the four other suggested projects. Mechanical and electrical energy are treated in [Physics 9](https://www.lehrplanplus.bayern.de/fachlehrplan/gymnasium/9/physik). The spring worksheet specifies spreadsheet columns and a reference line, and the lens app presents the construction and comparison of distances without requiring the thin-lens equation from learners.

The curriculum calls for at least four of the six common student experiments to be carried out by learners. Classroom demonstrations cover any of the six that remain. Digital plots, circuit and ray diagrams, worksheet prompts and notebook fields support these activities. Section 4.1 also calls for hands-on NTG experiments with solar modules and other sources.

## Legacy card proposal

The [proposal](../physik-gymnasium-8/replaces-de-by.json) inventories the 24 items in the six legacy cards sets from Grades 7–9. Its fixture hashes refer to the exact bytes in zam-os/zam. The Grade 7 package already lists its two source sets as keep-active; the Grade 8 proposal identifies four candidates there because the current curriculum teaches the corresponding retrieval in Grade 8. Three candidates come from the Grade 9 electric-circuit and dynamics sets. The Grade 8 energy, machines and thermodynamics sets produce no candidates. Multi-part cards with a different scope and cards outside the checked Grade 8 scope account for the 17 keep-active entries. There are no category B proposals. Maintainer approval is pending for the seven category A proposals, including ownership of the neighbouring-grade items.

The 20 new atoms each bind to their exact section and link to a stable lesson anchor. Only NTG atoms bind a track. The 60 new cards carry no history-transfer or retirement instructions.
