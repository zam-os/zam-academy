# History, Gymnasium Grade 8

The package follows the [Bavarian Grade 8 history curriculum](https://www.lehrplanplus.bayern.de/fachlehrplan/gymnasium/8/geschichte).
The live page retrieved on 2026-11-28 supplies a single course for all
Gymnasium tracks. It identifies six learning areas and 36 expectations. Each
entry in the [coverage matrix](../geschichte-gymnasium-8/coverage.json) matches
one expectation in the live HTML after whitespace normalisation. The matrix
stores the SHA-256 of the fetched HTML. The 25 lessons give every expectation
an entry and distinguish between app practice and supported external work.

The course revisits absolutism from [Grade 7](https://www.lehrplanplus.bayern.de/fachlehrplan/gymnasium/7/geschichte)
when introducing Enlightenment ideas. The historical sequence ends with the
postwar settlement. Weimar's institutions and the November Revolution belong
to [Grade 9](https://www.lehrplanplus.bayern.de/fachlehrplan/gymnasium/9/geschichte).
The Bavarian longitudinal unit intentionally crosses both boundaries as
prescribed and includes territorial change after 1945.

The cartographic activities use selected locations and affiliations. The
learners can switch views, read the key and explain what changed. Work with
full historical boundaries and wartime fronts requires a history atlas.
The painting, the documentary extracts and the employment chart have source
notes. Learners can extend the monument and wartime remembrance workshops
through local research and classroom discussion.
