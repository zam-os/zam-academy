# Grade 7 Gymnasium French curriculum and card inventory

The package follows the Bavarian Grade 7 LehrplanPLUS for [first foreign language](https://www.lehrplanplus.bayern.de/fachlehrplan/gymnasium/7/franzoesisch/1-fremdsprache) and [second foreign language](https://www.lehrplanplus.bayern.de/fachlehrplan/gymnasium/7/franzoesisch/2-fremdsprache). The sources were read on 23 November 2026. Neither subject curriculum specifies hours per learning area. The source snapshots, hashes and 103 expectations and topic entries are recorded in [coverage.json](../franzoesisch-gymnasium-7/coverage.json). The curriculum check verifies the 49 first-language and 54 second-language entries in their respective sources after normalizing HTML entities and whitespace. Nested grammar lists stay with their parent entry.

The [Grade 6 first-language](https://www.lehrplanplus.bayern.de/fachlehrplan/gymnasium/6/franzoesisch/1-fremdsprache) path already introduces relatives and the passé composé. The [Grade 6 second-language](https://www.lehrplanplus.bayern.de/fachlehrplan/gymnasium/6/franzoesisch/2-fremdsprache) path introduces basic direct object pronouns, which learners build on when choosing indirect pronouns in Grade 7. The Grade 7 app adapts suitable lessons from the earlier package and provides the two curricula as separate paths. The [Grade 8 first-language](https://www.lehrplanplus.bayern.de/fachlehrplan/gymnasium/8/franzoesisch/1fs) and [Grade 8 second-language](https://www.lehrplanplus.bayern.de/fachlehrplan/gymnasium/8/franzoesisch/2fs) sources were consulted for the next step. The Grade 7 lessons use en for partitive quantities and leave structures such as the plus-que-parfait and the inversion patterns of Grade 8 for later.

Rows marked `supported` name the preparation provided by the app and the activity still needed outside it. This includes real listening conditions, oral presentations, spontaneous conversation and interpreting, regional pronunciation, independent learning with external tools, and contemporary research. The app uses fictional event material for training. The official sources and their page titles are recorded in [source-checks.json](../franzoesisch-gymnasium-7/source-checks.json).
