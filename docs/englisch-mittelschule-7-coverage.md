# Grade 7 English, Bavarian Mittelschule

The [R7 curriculum](https://www.lehrplanplus.bayern.de/fachlehrplan/mittelschule/7/englisch/regelklasse) and [M7 curriculum](https://www.lehrplanplus.bayern.de/fachlehrplan/mittelschule/7/englisch/mittlere-reife-klasse) were checked on 26 November 2026. The original HTML snapshots, SHA-256 hashes, and track-specific alignment live in the [package coverage matrix](../englisch-mittelschule-7/coverage.json).

The official pages specify five learning areas and give no area-by-area hours. [Grade 6](https://www.lehrplanplus.bayern.de/fachlehrplan/mittelschule/6/englisch) introduces short instructions, the school and leisure contexts and shorter adjective comparison. Grade 7 reuses them with longer materials, comparison with more and most, and a stronger emphasis on inference. The [R8 curriculum](https://www.lehrplanplus.bayern.de/fachlehrplan/mittelschule/8/englisch/regelklasse) and [M8 curriculum](https://www.lehrplanplus.bayern.de/fachlehrplan/mittelschule/8/englisch/mittlere-reife-klasse) place productive going-to for intentions in Grade 8. The Grade 7 lesson therefore asks learners to recognise and interpret plans.

Natural speech with interference and audiovisual cues, spontaneous conversations, classroom presentations with feedback and independent research extend the app practice. The material samples the vocabulary. The curricula set year-long targets of about 300 new words and expressions in R7 and 350 in M7.
