# Grade 7 Realschule Biology coverage and legacy cards

The ISB served a single [Grade 7 Biology curriculum](https://www.lehrplanplus.bayern.de/fachlehrplan/realschule/7/biologie) on 2026-11-20. The candidate `wpfg1` and `wpfg2-3` URLs redirected to the LehrplanPLUS home page. The subject therefore has one path covering groups I, II, IIIa and IIIb.

[coverage.json](../biologie-realschule-7/coverage.json) matches the 38 verbatim statements to lessons, with evidence and the source SHA-256. Seven statements are marked as supported. Hands-on use of laboratory equipment, preparing slides, carrying out real experiments, building physical models, observing live unicellular organisms, researching actual plant structures as a class, and presenting to an audience remain classroom activities. The app provides planning, simulations, simplified diagrams and practice with example observations.

Grade 6 builds competence in scientific work, reproduction, vertebrates and local ecosystems. Grade 7 revisits how to investigate and uses the producer role as background for photosynthesis. The Grade 8 curriculum proceeds to fungi, bacteria and viruses, human immunity, human development and evolution. The Grade 7 app focuses on plant and animal cells, plant metabolism and the Grade 7 human organ systems. ## Legacy cells

[The mapping approved on 2026-11-20](../biologie-realschule-7/replaces-de-by.json) accounts for every item from the legacy Grade 7 plant and photosynthesis cell. On installation, cell version `2026.11.2` transfers each of four short retrievals (xylem, palisade tissue, the two photosynthesis inputs, and nighttime gas exchange) to one successor. Four extended prompts stay active because they span several new retrievals or demand additional material such as a balanced chemical equation. Since package version 0.1 the package is published (`publication.json` status `ready`) and listed in the catalog and atlas.

The other active legacy Grade 7 tile, on vertebrates and ecology, matches the current Grade 6 curriculum and is already inventoried in [the Grade 6 package](../biologie-realschule-6/replaces-de-by.json). The Grade 8 legacy digestion and circulation tiles cover some of the current Grade 7 content and remain in the [Grade 8 inventory](../biologie-realschule-8/replaces-de-by.json). The Grade 9 legacy nerves and senses tile remains in the [approved Grade 10 inventory](../biologie-realschule-10/replaces-de-by.json).
