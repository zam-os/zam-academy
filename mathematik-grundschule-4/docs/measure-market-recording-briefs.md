# Measure Market: recording handoff

Draft scripts, not teacher sign-off or recordings. Each German clip needs
reviewed captions, matching transcript, poster, rights for online/offline
use, and deliberate playback. Use the same models and units as the app.
Target 2–3 minutes each, with a real physical demonstration. Do not present
screen pixels as an accurately sized ruler. No pupil recording is needed.

| Lesson | Demonstration and German script | Review focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `laengen-messen` | Show a real ruler at zero, then a shifted band. „Das Band beginnt bei 13 mm und endet bei 58 mm. Seine Länge ist die Differenz: 45 mm, also 4 cm 5 mm. Ein Meter hat 100 Zentimeter.“ Demonstrate reading from above, then three bands plus reserve. | Ten intervals per cm; zero mark versus plastic edge; mm/cm/m/km; decimal metres; model versus actual measurement. |
| `masse-fassungsvermoegen` | Weigh an empty container, then content. „Die Kiste zählt mit, wenn wir alles zusammen wiegen. Masse messen wir in kg und g. Den Saftinhalt messen wir dagegen in l und ml.“ Pour 1 l into two 500-ml and four 250-ml portions. „Ein Viertel ist einer von vier gleich großen Teilen.“ | No universal kg/l equivalence; tare; level jug and eye height; half, quarter and three quarters; safe water activity. |
| `uhrzeit-zeitspannen` | Move both hands on a teaching clock. „Von 10:45 Uhr bis 12:10 Uhr vergehen 15, dann 60, dann 10 Minuten: 85 min oder 1 h 25 min.“ Demonstrate backward planning and 23:50 to 00:15 next day. | Hour hand moves continuously; 12/24-hour distinction; time versus duration; 60 s/min, 60 min/h, 24 h/day. |
| `geld-einkaufen` | Exchange play coins. „Mehr Münzen bedeuten nicht mehr Wert. 3 € 5 ct sind 3,05 €. Drei Hefte zu je 1,35 € kosten 4,05 €. Von 5 € bleiben 95 ct.“ Check the inverse addition; use a price list. | Zero cent place, equal value, count before change, same goods/no discount, exact budget comparison; no actual purchases. |
| `einheiten-wechseln` | Group labelled measurement blocks. „4 600 m sind 4 km 600 m. Die Strecke bleibt gleich. 1 kg sind 1 000 g, 1 l sind 1 000 ml. Zeit hat andere Schritte.“ Reverse a conversion and split a quantity into equal portions. | Correct domain before factor; whole large units and smaller remainder; no general decimal algorithm or formal Dreisatz. |
| `schaetzen-sachprobleme` | Show a known reference, estimate, then measure. „Aus ungefähren Angaben folgt ein ungefähres Ergebnis. Für 13 Kinder mit je 250 ml brauchen wir 3 250 ml: vier volle Literflaschen.“ Contrast with a purchase missing 5 ct. | Explain reference and uncertainty; sufficient whole packs; exact versus approximate question; physical work is not verified by app. |

Before production, the primary teacher checks the progression, everyday
reference sizes, language, accepted answers and physical materials. Record
review changes and actual reviewer/date in the media register only after
review. Leave all recording and release gates pending until assets exist.
