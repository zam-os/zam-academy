# Offline map data

Made with Natural Earth. Admin 0 – Countries, 1:110 million, version 5.1.1.

- [Original dataset](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/v5.1.1/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson).
- Original SHA-256: `6866c877d39cba9c357620878839b336d569f8c662d3cfab4cb1dbe2d39c977f`.
- [Public-domain terms](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/).
- Only names, codes and geometry retained. Boundaries reflect this dated small-scale dataset. No online map service or runtime requests.
- World display: equirectangular, with substantial polar area distortion. Germany display: longitude scaled by the cosine of its central latitude. Neither display is suitable for precise distance measurements.
- Capitals, approximate teaching coordinates and thematic example points are editorial annotations, not measured current statistics. State and district boundaries are not drawn.
