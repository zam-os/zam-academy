# Map data

Made with Natural Earth.

- Dataset: Admin 0 – Countries, 1:110 million, version 5.1.1.
- Upstream: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/v5.1.1/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson
- Original SHA-256: `6866c877d39cba9c357620878839b336d569f8c662d3cfab4cb1dbe2d39c977f`.
- Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/ — public domain.
- Extract: European features plus Türkiye and Cyprus; only names, administrative codes and geometry retained. No online map service or runtime requests.
- Boundaries reflect this dated small-scale dataset, not a statement about legal recognition. The EU list is maintained separately at the editorial date.
- Equirectangular display with latitude correction at the central parallel; neither equal-area nor suitable for precise distance measurements. Overseas areas are clipped to the European viewport. Tiny states may be represented by separate orientation markers.
